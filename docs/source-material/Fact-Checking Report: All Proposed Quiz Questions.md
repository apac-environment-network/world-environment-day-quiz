### ✅ **1. Genesis of Global Environmental Action**

**Q1:** Which country originally proposed the establishment of World Environment Day?  
**Verified Answer:** Japan.  
**Source Proof:** The World Environment Day official history states: "The UN General Assembly designated 5 June as World Environment Day... following a proposal by Japan."  
➡️ [World Environment Day – About / History](https://www.worldenvironmentday.global/about/history)

**Q2:** Under Japanese domestic law, how is the observance expanded?  
**Original Answer:** _The entire month of June is legally designated as "Environment Month" under the Basic Environment Law._  
**⚠️ Issue / Correction:**
- The **Basic Environment Law (1993)** designates **June 5** as Environment Day, not the entire month.
- The **Cabinet** decided in **1991** to designate June as "Environment Month" for promotional activities, but that is not codified in the Basic Environment Law.
- More accurate answer: **June 5 is legally established as Environment Day under the Basic Environment Law; additionally, June was designated as Environment Month by Cabinet decision to promote continuous public engagement.**  
- For a quiz, a concise answer could be: **"June 5 is designated as Environment Day, and the entire month of June is observed as Environment Month under government directive."**  
**Source Proof:**
- Ministry of the Environment (Japan) official information on Environment Day and Environment Month: [MOE – Environment Month](https://www.env.go.jp/en/season/envmonth/) (indicates month-long activities without attributing to Basic Environment Law)
- Outline of Basic Environment Law (only covers the Day): [MOE – Basic Environment Law](https://www.env.go.jp/en/laws/policy/basic/index.html)

> **Recommendation:** Adjust the quiz question to avoid legal inaccuracy: e.g., "How has Japan expanded the observance of World Environment Day beyond a single day?" Answer: "June is designated as Environment Month and June 5 as Environment Day."
